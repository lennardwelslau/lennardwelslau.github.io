\section{Research}

\subsection{Work in progress}

\pub{Bank Credit Supply and Entrepreneurship}{with Tobias Renkin and Filip Rozsypal\newline
{\small We study how bank credit supply at founding affects firm entry and performance. We match credit
supply shocks at Danish banks to potential entrepreneurs through their pre-existing personal banking relationship.
A one standard deviation increase in credit supply raises the rate at which individuals start firms by 3\%, and
firms founded under higher credit supply have higher revenue and employment for most of the following decade.}}
